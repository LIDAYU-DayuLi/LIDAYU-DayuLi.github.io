About

I am a Ph.D. student in Intelligent Information Technology at Westlake University. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Nankai University in 2026.

My research focuses on image restoration and embodied intelligence, including adverse-weather restoration with state-space models, Transformer architectures for low-level vision, and vision-language-action (VLA) policies for robots.
