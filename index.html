About

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Intelligent Information Technology at Westlake University (from Sept. 2026). I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Nankai University in 2026.

My research sits in image restoration and embodied intelligence: adverse-weather restoration with state-space models, Transformer architectures for low-level vision, and vision-language-action (VLA) policies for robots. I am currently a research intern on embodied AI at Haocun Technology (Hangzhou).
